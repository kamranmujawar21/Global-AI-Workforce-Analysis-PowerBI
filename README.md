# Global AI Workforce Analysis Dashboard

---

# Project Overview

This project analyzes the global AI job market from 2020–2026 to understand hiring trends, salary patterns, job demand, experience requirements, and work arrangements.

The dashboard was developed in Microsoft Power BI using DAX measures, data modeling, interactive slicers, and visualizations to transform raw job market data into meaningful insights.

Dataset Link - https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mann14/global-ai-and-data-science-job-market-20202026

---

# Business Objective

The objective is to analyze global AI employment trends, identify high-demand job roles, compare salaries across countries and industries, and explore opportunities for professionals at different experience levels.

---

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Total Job Records | Total AI job opportunities |
| Total Countries | Number of countries analyzed |
| Total Industries | Number of industries hiring AI professionals |
| Job Categories | Total AI job categories |
| Average Salary | Average salary across job listings |
| Fresher Jobs | Number of entry-level opportunities |

# Key Insights

- Australia offers the highest average AI salaries.
- Technology is the leading AI hiring industry.
- The global AI job market offers 16,648 entry-level opportunities.
- AI hiring remained consistently strong between 2020 and 2026.
- AI opportunities are available across multiple countries and industries.
